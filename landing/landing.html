The connections are the point

{{connection.title}}

{{connection.body}}
  1. In your stream

    Choose the dates.

    Booking a place to stay can wait until you know when you are going. The stream brings forward the next useful step.

  2. In your library

    Bring the pieces together.

    A coastal walk, a friend's recommendation, a note from a video. Open the trip to find them together, with links back to where they came from.

  3. After you get home

    The plan becomes a memory.

    Your photos join the places, people and days of the trip. You can find them again from any of those connections.
