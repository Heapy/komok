# Komok

## Hero

Your plans, knowledge and memories, connected.

Komok is a personal home for the things you want to do, understand and keep. Your day, your notes, your photos and the connections between them.

## Stream

Start with what needs your attention.

A birthday coming up. A task you can finally act on. Something new in a topic you follow. Open an item and the relevant notes, people and plans are right there.

## Library

Find the whole story.

Browse everything you keep as a table, a collection of documents or a gallery. Follow a connection from a place to a trip, from a photo to a person, from an idea to its source. The same information, wherever you need it.

## Connection

A trip is more than a task on a list.

It has dates to choose, places you have saved, people coming with you and things you want to remember. In Komok, they belong together.

## Knowledge

Keep the idea. Keep the source.

An article, a video or a podcast can add to what you already know. Komok is being built to find the useful ideas, connect them to your notes and show what is new. You can correct the result and return to the original passage.

## Assistant

Help with the organising.

Ask which saved places belong on your trip, or turn a goal into a plan. An assistant can help you organise the information and create useful views, without writing code. Use a local model or a provider you choose.

## Ownership

A personal archive, on your terms.

Keep what you need available offline. Build a photo archive your family can return to. Choose exactly what to share, with whom, and when.

## Invitation

Built around everyday life.

Komok starts with real, personal needs: planning a day, remembering an idea, finding a photograph. It is still in development. The prototype is a way to explore how these things can work together.
